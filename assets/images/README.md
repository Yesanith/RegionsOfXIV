# Plugin installer images

These are **not** compiled into the plugin. They are uploaded alongside
`manifest.toml` when submitting to
[DalamudPluginsD17](https://github.com/goatcorp/DalamudPluginsD17), in an
`images/` folder next to the manifest.

## `icon.png` (required)

- **1:1 aspect ratio**
- Between **64×64** and **512×512**

**Hand-make it.** The Dalamud AI policy explicitly prefers "a crude MS Paint icon
over an AI-generated icon," and the approval team may ask you to replace one. If
you'd rather not draw, ask in the [Dalamud Discord](https://discord.gg/holdshift) —
people volunteer for this.

## `image1.png` … `image5.png` (optional)

Up to five preview shots shown in the plugin installer. A zone-notification plugin
lives or dies on its screenshots — capture the notification mid-reveal, in an
attractive zone, at a sensible resolution.
